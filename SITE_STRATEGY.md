# Site strategy

Primary outcomes: calls, estimate requests and service requests. The visitor should identify the business, work, Vernon location and contact path within the first viewport.

## Journey

1. Hero: business wordmark, clear heating/cooling message, locality, since-1995 evidence, call and estimate actions.
2. Services: confirmed AC, heating, replacement and maintenance. Residential/commercial scope is stated without unsupported equipment promises.
3. Problem section: four common customer situations, without diagnosing faults. Each request link preselects the relevant service.
4. Why Huber: directory-supported longevity, physical local address and residential/commercial work.
5. Reviews: a dated aggregate and attributed historical excerpts linked to the full listing, including critical feedback.
6. Service area: Vernon location, with a call-to-confirm invitation for locations beyond Vernon.
7. Request: minimal form, honest email-draft workflow, direct phone fallback.
8. Contact: phone, email, address and qualified listed office hours.
9. Final call action and independent-concept disclosure.

## Conversion design

Calls are the first action because immediate availability requires a conversation. Request links use an on-page form rather than an external lead marketplace. Estimate links preserve estimate intent in the email subject. Data is validated locally. Preparing a draft does not trigger transmission, claim success or reserve an appointment. The second step explicitly opens the visitor's email client.

The mobile bar offers one call action and one request action, without a floating stack of redundant widgets. Service selection uses the visitor's language. Details can be shared without knowing the technical diagnosis. The site has no analytics or third-party tracking in this concept.

## SEO and preview treatment

Semantic landmarks, one h1, descriptive title/description, basic Open Graph and minimal HVACBusiness JSON-LD are included. The schema has only a name, phone and address, and does not claim the concept URL is Huber's official website. No review schema, license data, emergency hours, pricing or partner brands are fabricated. noindex, nofollow and a concept footer are deliberate preview protections.
