# Self-critique

The concept makes the phone number, service scope and Vernon location easy to find, has no third-party load dependencies, and provides honest request behavior. Evidence is used instead of invented business claims. The image-free treatment avoids fabricated proof but is less personal than a site built from authentic owner-approved photography.

The limitation with greatest conversion impact is the mailto flow: it depends on the visitor's configured email app and does not provide receipt, delivery confirmation or a scheduling queue. A staffed, privacy-aware submission endpoint would be preferable for an official launch. Direct calls remain available throughout.

Directory-sourced facts and historical reviews are adequate for a bounded proposal, but are weaker than owner-confirmed current facts and directly verified current reviews. Several listings may syndicate the same material. The year 1995 is corroborated across listings, but continuous current operation is not independently proven. Office hours conflict in one directory and are qualified on page. The selected excerpts are old and intentionally labeled historical; they should not be presented as recent customer evidence.

The original wordmark/palette may need revision after brand approval. The request form is deliberately a proposal feature; no claim is made that Huber has accepted it. The public preview can be shared but is not confidential. Search-engine indexing controls are advisory.
