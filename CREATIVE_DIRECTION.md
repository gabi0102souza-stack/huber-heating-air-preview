# Creative direction

Visual thesis: an established local trades business, expressed through bold signage-like type, clean rules and a practical service notice.

The concept uses graphite #222621, near-white #fafaf7, muted olive-gray text and a mustard #efbd55 accent. The deeper colors and sharply divided layout evoke business stationery and a shop sign. This is a proposed identity, not a recreation of an existing approved Huber logo.

The hero states the work and Vernon location immediately. A dark typographic service panel provides a memorable visual without implying ownership of a photographed team or installation. The wordmark is plain text. No gradients, flame/snowflake artwork, manufactured badges, stock staff, SaaS dashboard styling or gratuitous animation are used.

Typography relies on system fonts to avoid font requests and layout shifts. Headings are large enough to scan, while service descriptions remain specific. Georgia is reserved for short customer quotations. Service categories use rows rather than a wall of cards. The problem section uses one shared ruled grid. The form has a light surface to separate the task from the surrounding text.

At narrow widths, columns stack, the telephone stays visible and a two-action bottom bar preserves access to calls and requests. Keyboard focus is explicit. Reduced motion disables smooth scrolling.
